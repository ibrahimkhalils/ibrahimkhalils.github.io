This research investigates how stable isotope analysis can distinguish tomato farming systems and reveal environmental influences. The study demonstrates that nitrogen isotope ratios (δ¹⁵N) provide evidence of farming practices, while carbon isotope ratios (δ¹³C) reflect environmental background conditions.

By combining isotope signatures with agricultural and environmental interpretation, this work contributes to improved food traceability, authentication of production systems, and understanding of plant–environment interactions.
